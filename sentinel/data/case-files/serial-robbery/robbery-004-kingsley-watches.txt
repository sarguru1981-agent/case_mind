---

INCIDENT REPORT

At 08:15 AM on 2 April 2025, Mr David Kingsley arrived to open Kingsley
Watch Co. and discovered the rear delivery entrance had been opened. The
securing bar on the internal side had been removed and placed against the
wall. No alarm activation had been recorded by the monitoring station
overnight. Mr Kingsley immediately called 999. Officers attended at 08:29 AM.

Kingsley Watch Co. is a specialist retailer and authorised service centre for
high-value mechanical watches. The premises holds significant stock of luxury
timepieces in display cabinets and a secure stockroom.

---

STOLEN PROPERTY

The following items are confirmed missing:

  - Fifty-four luxury wristwatches from the main showroom display (Audemars
    Piguet, Vacheron Constantin, A. Lange & Söhne, Patek Philippe):
    estimated retail value £218,000
  - Watch service parts and movements from the workshop:
    estimated value £6,400

Total confirmed missing: £224,400

This is the highest-value loss recorded in the Operation Nightfall series
to date. Display cabinets were opened using the correct keys. No forced entry
was identified on the cabinet locks themselves.

---

FORENSIC FINDINGS

Officers recovered no usable latent fingerprints from the cabinets or the
rear delivery entrance. A cotton fibre sample consistent with a work garment
was recovered from the securing bar.

Alarm system investigation: Kingsley Watch Co. uses the SentryGuard system.
Panel logs show the system entered maintenance mode at 23:44 on 1 April 2025.
The credential used was NF-3847. The panel remained in maintenance mode until
05:22 AM on 2 April. The maintenance mode window of approximately five hours
and thirty-eight minutes encompassed the estimated period of entry and removal
of stock.

Access log records from Northstar Facilities Ltd, obtained under the ongoing
Major Crimes enquiry, show that credential NF-3847 was used to enter the
Kingsley Watch Co. premises at 23:51 on 1 April 2025. This access event
occurred after the alarm was placed into maintenance mode. The credential
record identifies NF-3847 as assigned to the engineer D. MERCER.

Northstar Facilities Ltd records show that D. MERCER attended Kingsley Watch
Co. on 29 March 2025 for a scheduled maintenance visit, three days before
the burglary. The visit was for the SentryGuard panel and general building
services.

---

INVESTIGATION NOTES

The identification of credential NF-3847 as assigned to D. MERCER, and its
use to gain access to the premises on the night of the burglary, represents
the strongest evidential link established in the Operation Nightfall series
to date.

Enquiries have confirmed that D. MERCER is Daniel Mercer, aged 34, of an
address in Southfield, Millbrook. Mercer is a qualified facilities engineer
employed by Northstar Facilities Ltd. His employment record at Northstar
Facilities shows no prior disciplinary actions and generally positive
performance reviews.

Officers note that Northstar Facilities Ltd itself operates legitimately.
The suspected criminal activity involves an individual abusing the access
and technical knowledge conferred by legitimate employment. No evidence has
emerged implicating any other Northstar Facilities employee.

Cross-referencing Daniel Mercer's credential access records with all four
Operation Nightfall premises confirms he conducted maintenance visits
to each location in the three days preceding the respective burglary.

A detailed financial investigation into Daniel Mercer has been initiated
in parallel. Results pending.

---

CONCLUSION

Investigation active. Daniel Mercer is the primary individual of interest.
Application for arrest warrant under review pending financial enquiry results.
Operation Nightfall series investigation ongoing.
