---

INCIDENT REPORT

At 06:22 AM on 15 January 2025, the proprietor of Hawthorne Jewellers, Mr
Edmund Hawthorne, discovered the premises had been entered overnight. Officers
attended at 06:40 AM. The rear service door, which opens onto a shared delivery
lane, showed signs of forced entry at the deadbolt. No alarm activation was
recorded by the monitoring station for the SentryGuard system installed on site.

Internal CCTV was inoperable at the time of the incident. Hawthorne Jewellers'
installation records show the camera system was last serviced 11 weeks prior
and had experienced intermittent recording failures.

No witnesses reported hearing or seeing anything until the proprietor's arrival.

---

STOLEN PROPERTY

The following items are confirmed missing:

  - Loose diamond inventory (certified stones, mixed cuts): estimated value £47,200
  - Seventeen luxury wristwatches (Rolex, Patek Philippe, and Omega):
    estimated retail value £31,400
  - Small amount of petty cash from till drawer: £200

Total estimated loss: £78,800 (pending full stock audit by insurance assessor)

The main display cases were opened cleanly — no glass broken. Access to the
display cases required knowledge of, or the physical keys for, a secondary
locking mechanism. The till drawer was forced.

---

FORENSIC FINDINGS

Fingerprint examination returned no usable latent prints at the point of
forced entry. Trace material recovered from the rear service door frame
included synthetic fibres consistent with a work or maintenance garment.

A partial boot impression was recovered from soil adjacent to the rear entrance,
estimated size 10–11 (UK). Cast taken; no match identified to date.

Alarm system investigation: SentryGuard technical logs show the panel entered
maintenance mode at 23:11 on 14 January 2025, triggered by a correctly
authorised credential. The panel remained in maintenance mode until 04:47 AM
on 15 January 2025, providing a window of approximately five hours and
thirty-six minutes during which no alarm events were recorded or transmitted
to the monitoring station.

SentryGuard customer support confirmed that placing a panel into maintenance
mode suppresses all motion detection and perimeter alerts. This action is
available to engineer-level credential holders only.

---

PREMISES VISIT RECORDS

Management at Hawthorne Jewellers confirmed a scheduled security system
maintenance visit took place on 11 January 2025 — three days before the
burglary. The visit was arranged through Northstar Facilities Ltd, a contracted
facilities management company used by several commercial properties in the
Millbrook area.

Northstar Facilities confirmed the visit and provided a work order reference.
The attending engineer conducted a routine inspection and firmware update to
the SentryGuard control panel and sensors. A second individual was present with
the attending engineer but was not named on the primary work order.

The premises manager at the time did not ask the second individual for
identification and described them as wearing a dark grey utility jacket.
The manager confirmed both individuals appeared professional and were not
challenged.

---

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Enquiries are ongoing into the identity of the second individual present
during the Northstar Facilities maintenance visit on 11 January 2025.

A witness on Calloway Street reported seeing a dark blue commercial van
parked in the delivery lane behind the premises at approximately 01:30 AM on
15 January 2025. The witness was unable to identify a registration plate.
The van was not observed by any CCTV system. No further description is
available.

Three members of staff at Hawthorne Jewellers have provided statements.
All deny involvement. Background checks returned no prior offending. No
connection to any individual of interest has been established.

The proprietor confirms the secondary locking keys for the display cases are
kept in a locked cabinet in the back office. Officers have been unable to
determine how the cases were opened without signs of forced entry. The
possibility of key duplication during a prior access event has not been
ruled out.

---

CONCLUSION

Investigation active. No suspect charged. Enquiries continuing into the
premises visit records and the identity of the unregistered second individual
present on 11 January 2025.
