INCIDENT REPORT

At 07:05 AM on 9 February 2025, gallery director Ms Priya Anand arrived to
prepare for an opening and discovered a ground-floor fire exit had been opened
from inside the building. Officers attended at 07:18 AM.

The gallery's SentryGuard alarm system had not transmitted any alert to the
monitoring station overnight. Initial review by the attending officer noted
that no activation record existed for the previous night, consistent with the
system having been in a non-operational state during the period of entry.

Internal CCTV recorded footage up to approximately 23:01 on 8 February,
after which recording ceased. Gallery management confirmed no scheduled
maintenance or power work was planned for that evening.

---

STOLEN PROPERTY

The following works are confirmed missing:

  - "Fractured Light" (oil on canvas, 2021) by Yael Ostroff: insured value £38,000
  - "Nocturne No. 4" (mixed media on board, 2023) by Yael Ostroff: insured value £29,500
  - "Study in Burnt Sienna" (oil on canvas, 2019) by Thomas Redfield: insured value £27,600

Total confirmed missing: £95,100

Two additional works were moved from their hanging positions and left on the
floor near the fire exit. These were undamaged. The significance of the
selection, if any, is not yet established.

---

FORENSIC FINDINGS

No useful latent fingerprints were recovered from the fire exit push bar or
the internal exhibition space. Cotton fibre traces and a faint impression of
a non-slip boot sole were recovered near the fire exit threshold.

Alarm system investigation: SentryGuard technical logs show the control panel
entered maintenance mode at 22:58 on 8 February 2025, following an authorised
engineer credential entry. The panel remained in maintenance mode until 05:33
AM on 9 February 2025. CCTV recording loss at 23:01 occurred approximately
three minutes after the alarm entered maintenance mode.

SentryGuard customer support confirmed the gallery's alarm is on the same
contract managed through Northstar Facilities Ltd. The maintenance mode entry
on 8 February was preceded by a legitimately registered credential.

A white transit van was recorded on a council CCTV camera at the junction of
Fenwick Lane and Beaumont Street at 01:47 AM on 9 February. The registration
plate was captured clearly as RGW-7734. Officers ran a check: RGW-7734 is
registered to H&T Bakeries Ltd, a commercial bakery operating out of Stokebridge
Industrial Estate. The vehicle is a known overnight delivery vehicle that uses
Fenwick Lane regularly. The driver confirmed a scheduled delivery to a restaurant
further along the lane; CCTV corroborates his account. H&T Bakeries Ltd has no
connection to the gallery.

---

PREMISES VISIT RECORDS

Millbrook Contemporary Gallery records confirmed a scheduled maintenance
visit by Northstar Facilities Ltd on 5 February 2025 — three days before
the burglary. The visit covered the alarm system and internal lighting rig.

The gallery receptionist who was present during the visit described the
attending engineer as wearing a grey maintenance jacket. The engineer was
alone for approximately fifteen minutes in the plant room where the alarm
control panel is located. The receptionist confirmed she did not accompany
the engineer during this period.

---

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Detective Okafor has formally noted the operational overlap with Case
MCR-2025-0101 (Hawthorne Jewellers, 14–15 January 2025). Both incidents
involved:

  — SentryGuard alarm placed into maintenance mode by an authorised credential
     a short period before each burglary
  — A Northstar Facilities Ltd maintenance visit three days prior to the incident
  — No alarm activation transmitted to the monitoring station

A request has been submitted to Northstar Facilities Ltd for a full list of
engineer credentials with access to SentryGuard maintenance mode functions
and records of all visits to properties in Millbrook in the preceding three
months. Northstar Facilities Ltd has acknowledged the request.

The white van sighted on Fenwick Lane (RGW-7734) has been confirmed as
unrelated to this investigation. Officers consider it eliminated as a
vehicle of interest.

No suspect has been identified. Enquiries continuing.

---

CONCLUSION

Investigation active. Detective Okafor treating Case MCR-2025-0148 and
Case MCR-2025-0101 as potentially linked. Formal series designation pending
review by Detective Inspector.
